Chili RAW culls, develops and catalogs your photos in one window on the Mac. This
manual covers what every control does; the chapters follow the app's own views.

Three things are true everywhere in it:

- **Your files stay put.** Chili RAW reads folders that already live on your disk —
  it never imports, copies, or moves your photos.
- **Your pixels are never overwritten.** Develop edits, Film Labor Looks, Pixels
  layers and versions are kept in small sidecar files beside your photos, and are
  rendered into pixels only when you export.
- **Metadata saves itself.** Ratings, flags, keywords, captions and GPS are staged
  as you work, then written a second or so after you stop — into the file itself or
  into an XMP sidecar, whichever you chose in **Settings ▸ Files ▸ Write target**.
  **⌘S** writes them right now rather than waiting, and it's the one that honours
  **Back up files before writing**.

Features that belong to the Pro version are marked **Pro** where they come up.
