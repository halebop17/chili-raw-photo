Chili RAW culls, develops and catalogs your photos in one window on the Mac. This
manual covers what every control does; the chapters follow the app's own views.

Three things are true everywhere in it:

- **Your files stay put.** Chili RAW reads folders that already live on your disk —
  it never imports, copies, or moves your photos.
- **Your pixels are never overwritten.** Develop edits, Film Labor Looks, Pixels
  layers and versions are kept in small sidecar files beside your photos, and are
  rendered into pixels only when you export.
- **Metadata saves itself.** Ratings, flags, keywords, captions and GPS are staged
  as you work, then written a second or so after you stop — into the file itself or
  into an XMP sidecar, whichever you chose in **Settings ▸ Files ▸ Write target**.
  **⌘S** writes them right now rather than waiting, and it's the one that honours
  **Back up files before writing**.

Features that belong to the Pro version are marked **Pro** where they come up.

## Contents

1. **[Getting started & the workspace](/manual/getting-started/)** — Start with Chili RAW on your Mac: add a folder, find your way around the folder tree and the seven views, and turn on People, Film Labor and Pixels.
2. **[Browsing & culling](/manual/browsing-and-culling/)** — Cull a shoot in the Grid: rate with stars, flag picks and rejects, sort, batch rename, and score the likely keepers, all from the keyboard.
3. **[The Loupe & the viewer](/manual/loupe-and-viewer/)** — Check focus at 1:1 with the Loupe and focus peaking, see where the camera focused, and compare photos side by side in the full-screen viewer.
4. **[Stacks](/manual/stacks/)** — Group bursts into stacks: set the threshold, expand and collapse a stack, and choose which frame stands for it in the Grid.
5. **[Develop](/manual/develop/)** — Edit RAW photos without touching the originals: tone, colour and curves, grading and LUTs, crop and lens corrections, repair, and masks.
6. **[Lift & Stamp](/manual/lift-and-stamp/)** — Copy an edit from one photo and stamp it onto others, all of it or only the parts you tick, in three steps.
7. **[Geo Tag](/manual/geo-tag/)** — Put photos on a map: click or search for a place, copy and paste GPS between photos, and write real GPS coordinates into your files.
8. **[EXIF & IPTC](/manual/exif-and-iptc/)** — Read your camera's EXIF data and write IPTC captions, keywords, creation dates and copyright, for one photo or many at once.
9. **[Catalog & search](/manual/catalog-and-search/)** — Search your photo library, build Smart Folders, and keep the catalog in step with the folders on disk.
10. **[People](/manual/people/)** — Find faces in your photos, name them, and see every photo of a person, with face recognition running entirely on your own Mac.
11. **[Pixels](/manual/pixels/)** — Add text, date stamps, shapes, frames, borders and paint on top of a photo, as layers that stay editable until you export.
12. **[Film Labor](/manual/film-labor/)** — The darkroom: pick a film stock, add halation and grain, print onto paper, and choose the light the print is viewed under.
13. **[Settings](/manual/settings/)** — Every preference in Chili RAW, tab by tab: files, backups, AI models, cache, updates and Shortcuts automation, and the keyboard reference.
14. **[Collections & backups](/manual/collections-and-backups/)** — Collections hold hand-picked sets of photos from any folder. Vaults back your photos and edits up to another drive.
15. **[Keyboard shortcuts](/manual/keyboard-shortcuts/)** — Every keyboard shortcut in Chili RAW on one page: culling, the Grid, the Loupe, Develop, Geo Tag and more.
