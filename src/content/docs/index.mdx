Chili RAW is a complete RAW photo workflow for the Mac. The grid is a full asset
manager: burst stacking, filters for rating, flag, media type, place and people,
Smart Folders, Collections, and search that finds a photo by filename, keyword,
place — or by what is in the picture. The develop side is a non-destructive RAW
pipeline with tone, curves, colour, local adjustments and masking, plus LUTs,
film-stock looks and a full film darkroom.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Three RAW decoders" icon="setting">
    Apple's CIRAWFilter, the Adobe DNG SDK, or LibRaw with your own DCP profiles —
    and three tone mappers to render them through. Compressed DNG included, JPEG XL
    and all.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cull a whole shoot" icon="approve-check">
    Bursts collapse into one tile, Compare and Survey share a zoom, a hover loupe
    checks focus without opening anything, and a Vision score orders the grid
    without touching a rating.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mask any part of a photo" icon="puzzle">
    Eleven kinds of mask that add, subtract and intersect. Feather softens a
    detected boundary; Edge moves it. Nothing leaves your Mac.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A real film darkroom" icon="star">
    Film stocks modelled from published datasheets, halation that happens inside
    the emulsion, grain sized per stock, and a paper and print stage.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
